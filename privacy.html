Privacy Policy

Your data

AutoBrain Shop processes job, vehicle and customer data you enter to run your workshop. Your business data is never sold and is never used to train models.

Customer information

Information about your customers — including the photos, work orders and approvals they see in the portal — belongs to your workshop. Access is limited to what the customer portal needs to show them their job.

Security & retention

Approvals are stored with a signed timestamp and audit trail so you stay covered. Data is held in Australian data centres and backed up as part of the service.
