Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before AutoBrain Shop launches.

When can I sign up?

AutoBrain Shop is launching soon. Join the waitlist and we'll email you the moment it goes live — no payment details needed.

How does the digital approval actually work?

When a job is ready for approval, your customer logs into the portal and sees the fault photos and itemised work order with the price. They approve with a tap. The approval is signed, timestamped and kept in the job's audit trail — so you're covered before you start the work.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. AutoBrain Shop is mobile-first and works in any browser — phone, tablet or the desktop at the counter. The customer portal is optimised for your customers' phones.

Does it work with Australian number plates?

Yes. Type a registration number and the vehicle details are pre-filled via the built-in Australian rego lookup.

Can I take payments at the counter?

Yes. Convert any estimate into an invoice and take payment at the counter by card, bank transfer or PayTo.

What is the annual option?

Buy a year upfront and pay for 11 months. Solo shop includes 2 hours of onboarding assistance; Team includes 10 hours of onboarding assistance to get your shop set up fast.

I run more than one workshop — is that supported?

Yes. The Multi-site / Own instance plan is built for multi-location shops and custom setups, with assisted onboarding. Email sales@autobrainservice.app for pricing.
