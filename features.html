Everything a workshop needs,
without the bloat

AutoBrain Shop is built around the job card and the approval — simple, fast and mobile-first, so your team spends less time typing and more time turning wrenches.

Planned 🔧

Job cards

Create a job in seconds — attach the vehicle and customer, add line items for labour and parts, and move it through the flow: Booked → In progress → Waiting approval → Approved → Done.

Planned ✍️

Digital customer approval

Your customer sees the live job, the fault photos and the itemised quote — then approves digitally with a signed, timestamped approval and a full audit trail. No more chasing "can you just sign off on this".

Planned 📷

Photo uploads

Upload fault photos to the job from your phone. Your customer sees them the moment they're uploaded — before they approve the work.

Planned 🧾

Estimates, invoicing & POS

Raise an estimate straight from the job card, convert it to an invoice and take payment at the counter — card, bank transfer or PayTo.

Planned 🚗

AU rego lookup

Enter a registration number and AutoBrain Shop pre-fills the vehicle details through the built-in Australian rego lookup. Less typing, fewer mistakes.

Planned 📦

Inventory management

Track parts and stock with a camera barcode scanner — scan parts in with your phone camera and keep your shelves accurate without a dedicated barcode reader.

Planned ⚠️

Low-stock alerts

Get alerted when you run low on stock, so you reorder before a job is waiting on a part.

Planned 🔔

Auto service reminders

Automatically remind customers to service their car after a set time — turn finished work into repeat bookings.

Planned 🔗

Customer car-link & history

Customers link their AutoBrain car instance, so you see all past work and AI diagnostics. Completed services auto-populate into AutoBrain in reverse order with receipts.

Planned 🔐

Customer portal

A clean, secure portal where each customer can log in, see their live job, photos, work order and price — and approve from their phone.
